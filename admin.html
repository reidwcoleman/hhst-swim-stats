Exporting Times from Swimtopia

Follow these steps to download swimmer times from Swimtopia, then drop the CSV in the upload zone below. Every row is saved to Firebase, so families instantly see the new times on the public site.

  1. 1

    Sign in to Swimtopia

    Go to swimtopia.com (or your team's .swimtopia.com subdomain) and sign in with your coach / admin account.

  2. 2

    Open the team site → Meets

    From your team dashboard, click Meets in the side navigation. You'll see every meet your team has competed in.

  3. 3

    Pick the meet you want to import

    Click the meet name. If results have been finalized in Meet Maestro, you'll see the Results tab become available.

  4. 4

    Open Results → Reports

    On the meet page, click Results, then Reports. Choose Individual Results (or Athlete Results) to get one row per swimmer per event.

  5. 5

    Export as CSV

    Click the Export button (or the download icon in the top right) and pick CSV (.csv). Save the file somewhere you can find it — your Downloads folder is fine.

  6. 6

    Drop it in the upload zone below

    Drag the CSV into the dashed box below, or click to browse. Times will be merged into matching swimmers and saved to Firebase automatically.

Tips for the cleanest import

  • Keep swimmer names consistent across meets. "Riley Carter" and "riley carter" will merge, but "Riley J. Carter" creates a separate swimmer.
  • Include at least name, event, time, meet, and date. age, group, and place are optional and just make the family dashboard richer.
  • If Swimtopia exports first name and last name as separate columns, that's fine — they're auto-combined.
  • Times can be SS.HH (28.42) or M:SS.HH (1:02.18). Both work — the seconds format is also stored for ranking.
  • Swimtopia exports don't include home addresses. After uploading, click Edit next to each swimmer below to add their address so families can look them up.
  • Re-uploading the same meet is safe. Matching swimmers merge; new races append.
  • Use the Download sample CSV button above to see the exact column layout the importer is happiest with.
